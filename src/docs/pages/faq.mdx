# FAQ

Short answers to common questions about Lumetrope.

## Is Lumetrope free and open source?

Yes. It is MIT-licensed. The bundled Poppins font is under the SIL Open Font License.

## Is it affiliated with LottieFiles?

No. Lumetrope is an independent implementation of the open Lottie and dotLottie 2.0 specifications, with no code, assets or branding from LottieFiles.

## What formats does it open and save?

It opens `.json`, `.lottie` and `.svg`. It saves dotLottie and Lottie JSON, and exports GIF, WebP and WebM. See [Exporting](/docs/exporting/).

## Where are my projects stored?

In your browser or the desktop app, on your device. There is no server. See [Projects and files](/docs/projects-and-files/).

## Does it work offline?

The desktop app does. The web app needs to load once.

## Can I make interactive animations?

Yes, with dotLottie 2.0 state machines. See [State machines](/docs/state-machines/).

## Can an AI agent use it?

Yes, through MCP. See [Connect AI agents with MCP](/docs/mcp/).
