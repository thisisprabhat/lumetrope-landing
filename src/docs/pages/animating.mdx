# Animating with keyframes and easing

In Lumetrope you can put keyframes on every property, shape each segment with a curve editor, and arrange them on the timeline. Presets give you common motion in one click.

## How do I add a keyframe?

Move the playhead to a frame, then change a property. Enable the stopwatch for that property to animate it. Keyframes appear as diamonds on the layer's row in the timeline, and you can drag them to retime.

## How do I change easing?

Open the curve editor for a keyframe pair, drag the bezier handles, or pick a preset. Use a hold key for a step change with no interpolation.

## What are motion paths and presets?

- **Motion paths:** animate position along an editable curve.
- **Motion presets:** fade, pop, slide, spin, bounce, shake and more.
- **Staggering:** offset the same motion across several layers.

## What does the timeline show?

Layer bars, keyframes and a segment (marker) band. Drag layer bars to change in and out points. See [Nested scenes and segments](/docs/scenes-and-segments/) for markers.
